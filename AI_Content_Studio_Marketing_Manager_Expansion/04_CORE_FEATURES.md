# Core Features

## Planning and Intelligence

- goal-based campaign wizard
- campaign proposals and calendars
- product/service/offer understanding
- channel and format recommendations
- scheduled and autonomous marketing
- experiment and A/B variant planning
- historical performance learning

## Creation

- captions, hashtags, CTA, descriptions
- images and edits
- Reels, short video, long video
- music, voice, sound effects
- soundscapes and albums
- thumbnails, artwork, derivatives
- SEO and email campaign text

## Execution

- reviews and revisions
- approvals
- content calendar
- publishing queues
- Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube
- distributor-ready audio releases
- exports for unsupported destinations

## Commerce

- product sync
- WooCommerce connector/plugin
- Shopify app
- product campaign generator
- offers, new arrivals, low-visibility products
- abandoned-cart content assets where lawful data and consent permit

## Control Plane

- MCP tools and agents
- OPA policies
- Temporal workflows
- audit trail
- secrets and platform permissions
- cost metering and credits
