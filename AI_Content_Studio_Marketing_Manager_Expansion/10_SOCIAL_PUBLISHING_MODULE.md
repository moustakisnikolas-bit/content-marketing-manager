# Social Publishing Module

## Initial Direct Targets

- Facebook Pages
- Instagram Professional
- TikTok
- YouTube and YouTube Shorts

## Expansion Targets Subject to API Approval

- Pinterest
- LinkedIn
- X
- Google Business Profile
- website/blog adapters

## Common Foundation

- developer app registration
- OAuth and encrypted tokens
- scope and account discovery
- effective-capability registry
- media validation
- publishing workers
- webhooks/polling
- rate limits and quotas
- idempotency and reconciliation
- analytics ingestion

## Platform Reality

Capabilities are enabled only when supported by the official API, connected account, granted scopes, app review status, and current platform restrictions.

## Unsupported Capability UX

Offer a safe fallback such as draft export, downloadable package, copy-ready content, or manual checklist. Never pretend a direct-publishing capability exists.
