# Analytics and Optimization

## Metric Areas

- impressions, reach, views
- likes, comments, shares, saves
- clicks and profile visits
- watch time and completion
- messages/bookings where integrations permit
- conversions, revenue, and ROAS where connected
- product/store outcomes

## Storage

Keep provider metric name/value plus valid normalized name, definition, unit, scope, measurement time, collection time, attribution window, currency, and raw metadata.

## Optimization Cycles

- content comparison
- campaign comparison
- platform comparison
- best time/format/tone analysis
- CTA and hashtag performance
- product/category visibility
- repurposing suggestions
- experiment outcomes

## Attribution Caution

The system must clearly distinguish observed platform metrics, tracked website/store events, attributed conversions, and inferred relationships. It must not imply causation without evidence.
