# Product Vision

## Strategic Positioning

### From

“Create me a post.”

### To

“Help me sell this product or service using the right content, timing, format, message, channel, and publishing plan.”

## Vision Statement

Build the easiest goal-based AI marketing operating system for a non-technical small business.

## Differentiators

- outcome-first rather than model-first,
- beginner guidance rather than technical configuration,
- content creation plus execution,
- marketing intelligence based on owned performance history,
- eCommerce product awareness,
- explainable recommendations,
- governed auto-pilot,
- multimedia creation including advanced audio albums,
- provider-neutral routing and measurable unit economics.

## North-Star Metric

Approved, successfully published, and measured campaign outputs per active customer.

Supporting metrics include:

- time from goal to campaign approval,
- first-preview acceptance,
- cost per accepted output,
- publication success,
- recommendation acceptance,
- attributable clicks/conversions where connected,
- retention and expansion.
