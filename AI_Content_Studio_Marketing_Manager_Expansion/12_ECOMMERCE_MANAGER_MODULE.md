# AI eCommerce Manager Module

## Commercial Forms

1. Native AI Content Studio module
2. WooCommerce plugin
3. Shopify app
4. Future connectors

## Synced Product Data

- title and description
- images and gallery
- price and discount
- stock status
- category, tags, SKU
- URL and variants
- new-arrival status
- best-seller/low-performing flags when source data permits

## Outputs

- product posts, Stories, Reels, videos, ads creative
- captions and hashtags
- product and SEO descriptions
- email campaign copy
- offer, launch, seasonal, and educational campaigns
- abandoned-cart content templates where lawful and consented
- product-priority recommendations

## Services

- StoreConnectionService
- ProductSyncService
- ProductIdentityResolver
- InventorySignalService
- StoreAssetSyncService
- CommerceCampaignService
- CommerceAnalyticsIngestionService
- CommerceRecommendationService

## Required Controls

- field-level sync configuration
- least-privilege scopes
- webhook authenticity
- idempotent imports
- deletion/uninstall handling
- consent and privacy rules
- data minimization
- per-store tenant isolation
- sync reconciliation and error recovery
