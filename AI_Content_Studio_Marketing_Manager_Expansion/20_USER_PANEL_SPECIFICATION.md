## Visual Direction

Use an original dark modern media interface with clean cards, green accent, simple navigation, friendly labels, and progressive disclosure. Do not copy Spotify branding or proprietary assets.

## Copy Standard

Prefer:

- Connect your store
- Choose what to promote
- Review your campaign
- Create previews
- Approve and schedule

Avoid:

- sync cursor
- job payload
- provider route
- OAuth scope identifier
- model token usage
