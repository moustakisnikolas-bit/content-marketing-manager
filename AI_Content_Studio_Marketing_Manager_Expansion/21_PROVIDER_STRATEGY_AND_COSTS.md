# Provider Strategy and Costs

## Provider-Neutral Ports

- text/vision/image/video/music/speech/sound effects
- object storage
- publishing and analytics
- store connectors
- music distribution
- payments

## Routing Factors

- capability
- quality and product fidelity
- cost per accepted output
- latency and reliability
- reference-media support
- data policy and region
- customer entitlement
- provider health
- maximum authorized cost

## Cost Registry

Every price snapshot records provider, model, operation, billing unit, currency, effective/captured dates, and source reference.

## Cost Categories

- AI and gateway
- media processing
- storage/bandwidth
- workers
- store/platform API operations
- observability/control-plane allocation
- payment fees
- support/refunds

## Guardrail

Public prices must be calibrated against observed P50/P75/P90 recipe cost and cost per accepted output. No provider list price is used directly as customer price.
