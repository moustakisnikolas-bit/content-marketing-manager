## Tier C — Hidden Diamond / Future Consumer Product

### Personal AI Memory / Life Manager

A future consumer product to build after AI Content Studio gains traction. Indicative concept pricing: EUR 2.99 to EUR 4.99 per month.

Positioning:

> A second brain for everyday life.

Potential memory categories include notes, screenshots, links, documents, bills, appointments, ideas, promises, birthdays, subscriptions, reminders, life events, family tasks, medicine reminders, home obligations, warranties, insurance dates, vehicle documents, tax reminders, and personal files.

This idea is explicitly excluded from the first AI Content Studio implementation.
