# AI Content Creation Module

## Inputs

- product/service text
- image or gallery
- store URL or website URL
- existing post or campaign
- video/audio
- logo and brand rules
- campaign goal and notes

## Outputs

- captions and hashtag sets
- product and SEO descriptions
- image posts and edits
- carousels and Stories
- short and long videos
- social advertisements as creative drafts
- email and blog copy
- music/audio and voiceovers

## Lifecycle

```text
brief -> recipe -> estimate -> preview -> quality gate -> review -> selected version -> final render -> package
```

## Version Rules

Distinguish:

- text edit,
- media edit,
- creative variant,
- complete regeneration,
- draft preview,
- final render,
- mastering.

Technical/provider failures do not consume a customer creative version. Extra valid variants are purchased through affordable add-ons.

## Brand Consistency

Every generation can consume a confirmed Brand Profile containing tone, vocabulary, colors, logos, visual rules, forbidden claims, CTAs, and target audiences.
