# Target Users

## Primary Personas

### Small-Business Owner

Needs practical marketing without learning marketing terminology, AI tools, design, editing, or APIs.

### Creator or Freelancer

Needs repeatable content, personal-brand campaigns, scheduling, and performance feedback.

### eCommerce Store Owner

Needs product synchronization, offer campaigns, new-arrival promotion, product imagery, short video, email copy, and performance prioritization.

### Local Business

Needs offers, events, bookings, messages, local awareness, reviews-related content, and simple campaign reminders.

### Agency

Needs multiple client workspaces, approvals, cost allocation, reusable recipes, team roles, reporting, and later white labeling.

## Persona Constraints

The product must support users with no knowledge of:

- marketing,
- AI,
- design,
- video editing,
- music production,
- social strategy,
- technical integrations,
- eCommerce automation.
